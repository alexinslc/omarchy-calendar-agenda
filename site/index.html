How it works

A small, deliberate connection.

The plugin asks only for the access it needs to identify connected accounts and render upcoming events.

01

Connect

Choose Connect Google Calendar and approve narrowly scoped, read-only access in your system browser.

02

View

Calendar Agenda synchronizes an approximately 28-day window for private day, week, and month views.

03

Disconnect

Remove an account from Settings to revoke access and immediately purge its locally cached calendar data.

Google user data

What is accessed—and why.

  • Account identity
    Email address and stable account identifier label accounts and prevent duplicate connections.
  • Calendar list
    Calendar identifiers, names, and colors let you recognize and filter subscribed calendars.
  • Upcoming events
    Titles, times, all-day status, and locations render the agenda for the upcoming sync window.
  • OAuth refresh token
    Stored in Linux Secret Service so background synchronization can continue without repeated consent.
Calendar Agenda does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, transfer it to an external service, or use it to train machine-learning models.
