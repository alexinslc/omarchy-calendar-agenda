How it works

One quiet icon. Your whole agenda.

Connect your Google accounts once, then stay in the flow without opening another browser tab.

01

Connect

Choose Connect Google Calendar and approve narrowly scoped, read-only access in your system browser.

02

Glance

Open day, week, or month views directly beneath the calendar icon. The active Omarchy theme comes with you.

03

Control

Filter calendars, add multiple accounts, sync on demand, or disconnect and purge local data from Settings.

Install

Add it with one command.

Calendar Agenda uses the Python, Secret Service, and systemd components already present in a standard Omarchy installation.

$omarchy plugin add https://github.com/alexinslc/omarchy-calendar-agenda.git --enable

Google user data

Only what the agenda needs.

No analytics, ads, remote calendar database, or server receiving Google user data.

  • Account identity
    Email address and stable account identifier label accounts and prevent duplicate connections.
  • Calendar list
    Calendar identifiers, names, and colors let you recognize and filter subscribed calendars.
  • Upcoming events
    Titles, times, all-day status, and locations render the agenda for the upcoming sync window.
  • OAuth refresh token
    Stored in Linux Secret Service so background synchronization can continue without repeated consent.
Calendar Agenda does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, transfer it to an external service, or use it to train machine-learning models.
